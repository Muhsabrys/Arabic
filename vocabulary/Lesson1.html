Cultural Insight

In Arabic-speaking cultures, greetings are very important and often involve asking about one's health and family. As-salamu alaykum (peace be upon you) is the most common Islamic greeting used throughout the day. The response Wa alaykum as-salam (and peace be upon you too) is equally important and should always be given.
